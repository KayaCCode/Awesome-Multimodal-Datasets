02 / A FIELD GUIDE

A dataset is more than its size.

01

Know the supervision.

Human annotation, measurements and generated labels carry different evidence. Generated answers are useful training signals, but are not independent ground truth.

02

Protect the evaluation.

Inspect splits and upstream sources. A dataset can support training and evaluation, but the examples used for each must remain separate.

03

Check the actual release.

Media may require separate retrieval. Verify the version, license, access process and conversion needed for your model.
